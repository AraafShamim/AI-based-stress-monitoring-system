About मनSetu

Technology that listens. Intelligence that cares.

Your mental wellbeing,
supported with care.

मनSetu is a digital mental health and wellbeing platform designed to help individuals understand their emotional wellbeing, access timely support, and connect with appropriate mental health resources.

By bringing together technology, wellbeing assessments, meaningful insights, trusted resources and human counselling, मनSetu aims to make mental health support more accessible and approachable.
